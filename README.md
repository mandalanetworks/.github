Mandala is a sovereign engine for your life and your business.

You write down how you work, once, in plain words. Everything else is built from it, and nothing can go around it. Not a person, not an app, not the AI.

### Highlights

- **Rules you can read.** Written in plain words, checked by a solver, enforced before anything happens.
- **An AI that can't break them.** It acts for you, inside your rules, and asks when it should.
- **Everything recorded.** Every action signed: who, for whom, under which rule.
- **Yours.** Runs on your own machines. No account with us.
- **Connected.** Your devices, and other businesses, talk to each other directly.

### Under the hood

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Rules** (Dharma) | A tiny language for business rules. Contracts are readable Markdown documents, checked with Z3, compiled to deterministic WebAssembly, versioned and dated. |
| **Record** (Karma) | A projection-first database engine written from scratch in C++. Tables, text, vectors, graphs, places and time series in one store, fed by signed facts. |
| **Knowledge** (Medha) | Documents as records, mirrored to plain Markdown, merged with CRDTs, edited together in real time. |
| **Mind** (Prajna) | Bring any AI model, or run one locally. It thinks with your context and acts only within your rules. |

Written in Rust and C++. One binary, one data directory, no external database or queue. Local first, with devices and businesses linked peer to peer.
